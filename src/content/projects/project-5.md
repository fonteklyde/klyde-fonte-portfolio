## Project Overview

Local government administration requires fast access to demographic statistics, household records, and community resident data. The **Barangay Profiling System** was built to digitize community record keeping for local government units.

By transitioning paper-based resident profiles into a secure web application, local officials can manage resident registrations, generate demographic reports, and issue official barangay certifications efficiently.

## Key Features

- 👥 **Resident & Household Management**: Comprehensive registration forms recording family compositions, employment status, sector classifications (Senior Citizens, PWDs, Solo Parents), and address mapping.
- 📜 **Official Certificate & Permit Issuance**: Automated generation of Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Indigency, and Residency forms.
- 📈 **Demographic Data Analytics**: Dynamic charts categorizing population age distributions, voter registration statistics, and employment metrics.
- 🔍 **Fast Resident Search & Verification**: Instant search filters by name, household ID, precinct number, or barangay zone.

## Technical Implementation

- **Framework**: Laravel PHP with MVC architecture for clean separation of concerns.
- **Database**: MySQL relational database schema optimized for demographic querying.
- **Frontend**: Custom HTML5/CSS3 styled with responsive layout principles for easy operation on desktop LGU computers.

## Impact & Results

Eliminated paper record loss, reduced certificate issuance turnaround time from hours to minutes, and provided local barangay officials with instant demographic reports for municipal planning.
