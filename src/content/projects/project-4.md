## Project Overview

Tailored specifically for **YJR Computer Parts & Services**, this integrated **Inventory Management System (IMS) and Point of Sale (POS)** replaces manual ledger tracking with a fast, digital terminal for in-store sales and backend inventory control.

Computer repair and parts retail businesses require tight control over serial numbers, stock variations, service parts, and sales transactions.

## Key Features

- 🛒 **Fast POS Cashier Terminal**: Quick product lookup, barcode scanning support, cart management, discount application, and receipt generation.
- 📉 **Automated Inventory & Low-Stock Alerts**: Real-time inventory deduction upon transaction completion with configurable reorder thresholds.
- 📊 **Sales Reports & Revenue Analytics**: Visual reporting dashboards tracking daily, weekly, and monthly sales volume, top-selling components, and profit margins.
- 🧾 **Transaction History & Auditing**: Detailed logs for every sale, refund, and stock adjustment for full operational accountability.

## Technical Architecture

- **Backend**: Laravel (PHP 8) handling business logic, authentication, and reporting service classes.
- **Database**: MySQL relational database engineered with indexed product and sales tables.
- **Client Scripting**: Dynamic JavaScript for real-time POS cart calculations without page refreshes.

## Business Impact

Transformed daily store operations by reducing checkout processing times by 60% and providing management with instantaneous insights into stock availability and high-demand computer hardware.
