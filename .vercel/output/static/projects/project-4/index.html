IMS and POS

An Inventory Management System (IMS) and Point of Sale (POS) system for YJR Computer Parts & Services using Laravel, PHP, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and MySql. The system manages product inventory, tracks stock levels, and processes sales transactions, helping streamline store operations and improve efficiency in monitoring products and sales records.
