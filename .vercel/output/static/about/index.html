About Me

Driven by Curiosity. Focused on Results.

I didn't start out as a developer — I started out as a tinkerer.

Growing up, I was the kid who took apart remote controls and gadgets just to see how they worked. That innate curiosity eventually led me to a keyboard, where I discovered that code is the ultimate toolkit for creation.

Today, I’m a Full-Stack Developer who thrives on untangling complex logic and turning abstract requirements into robust, intuitive software applications. For me, development isn't just about writing functions; it's about crafting tools that make people's lives easier and businesses run smoother.

Whether I'm optimizing database queries to shave milliseconds off load times, implementing client-side AI models, or crafting clean user interfaces, I believe that great software should feel effortless for the end user.
